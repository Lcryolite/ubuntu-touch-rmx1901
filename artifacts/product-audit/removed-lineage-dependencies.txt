Halium product dependency exclusions
====================================

Product definitions and inherited makefiles
-------------------------------------------
- device/realme/RMX1901/lineage_RMX1901.mk
  Not selected or inherited by halium_RMX1901.mk. This keeps its Evolution-era
  flags, mini-GApps selection, branding properties, and GMS client ID out of
  the Halium product.
- vendor/lineage/config/common_full_phone.mk
  Inherited only by lineage_RMX1901.mk; never inherited by halium_RMX1901.mk.
- hardware/dolby/dolby.mk
  In device.mk, inherited only when TARGET_PRODUCT is not halium_RMX1901.
- build/make/target/product/non_ab_device.mk
  Added to the newer ROM device tree for recovery applypatch support, but this
  makefile does not exist in Android 11 and the Halium product excludes OTA.
- hardware/qcom-caf/common/common.mk
  Added by the newer device tree, but the pinned Lineage 18.1 Qualcomm common
  project has no such product makefile; its os_pickup makefiles are discovered
  by the Android build system instead.
- vendor/qcom/opensource/healthd-ext/health-vendor-product.mk
  Added by a 2026 ROM-specific device-tree change. The pinned Halium 11
  manifest has no healthd-ext project, and the associated Lineage health
  service is excluded from the Halium product.

BoardConfig policy includes excluded from halium_RMX1901
--------------------------------------------------------
- device/lineage/sepolicy/libion/sepolicy.mk
  Added by a 2026 device-tree change; the pinned Lineage 18.1 sepolicy tree has
  no libion policy subtree.
- device/lineage/sepolicy/libperfmgr/sepolicy.mk
  Added during a 2024 ROM migration; the pinned Lineage 18.1 sepolicy tree has
  no libperfmgr policy subtree, and the associated Lineage power service is
  excluded from the Halium product.

No Evolution or GApps product makefile is inherited anywhere on the Halium
product path. The only Evolution/GApps settings present in the source tree are
isolated in lineage_RMX1901.mk, which remains unmodified.
